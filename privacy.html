Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers Clipping Pro, a private desktop application operated by FiveStar Clips. It is used only by its operator, on the operator's own computer, to manage the operator's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the operator signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to the operator's YouTube channel, read that channel's videos and comments, and read its YouTube Analytics reports. When the operator connects Instagram, the app requests permission to publish the operator's own Reels and read their insights and comments.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the operator's clips and to measure how those clips perform. The app makes no other use of it.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server run by FiveStar Clips.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. The app does not show ads and does not use the data for advertising.

Google API Services

Clipping Pro's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Deleting the app's data folder on the operator's computer removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions: through the About page of the FiveStar Clips YouTube channel.
